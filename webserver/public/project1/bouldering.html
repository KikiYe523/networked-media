One More Try



I like that the wall is honest.

It does not care about grades,
deadlines,
or everything waiting outside.

It only asks me
to reach,
to fall,
and to try again.




















The Best Part Is Who Comes With Me

My favorite part is not always reaching the top.

It is trying the same problem together,
sharing ideas from the ground,
laughing when we fall,
and hearing someone say,
“one more try.”




I climb for the movement.
I return for the people.
