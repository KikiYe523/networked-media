Sometimes Nothing Is Playing

I am not the biggest music enthusiast

sometimes I put these on
without choosing a song

they soften the traffic
the conversations
and everything asking for my attention

sometimes I want music
sometimes I only want
less noise



When I Do Press Play

When the noise is coming from inside
I listen to “Vienna”

usually when I am anxious
moving too quickly
or worried that I am falling behind

it reminds me
that not everything
has to happen at once
